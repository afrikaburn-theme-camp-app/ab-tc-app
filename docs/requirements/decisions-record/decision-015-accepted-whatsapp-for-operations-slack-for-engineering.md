# Decision 015: Use WhatsApp for operational messaging and Slack for engineering/discussion coordination
Date: 2026-08-07
Owner: Beyers Nel
Status: accepted
Related: [Decision 014](decision-014-proposed-governance-licensing-and-data-liability-thresholds-for-scaling-beyond-poc.md)

## Context

Group chat, 2026-08-07:
- 10:24 Beyers: "Agree with moving to Slack, if we are happy with the free tier."
- 13:31 Ryan James Noble: raised that Slack's free tier drops message history over time, that Discord is "chaotic," and flagged a possible NPO-grant route (via Pride Musvaire) to fund a paid tier.
- 13:37 Beyers: shared the Slack workspace invite.
- 18:55 Ryan James Noble (PSA): "We have a slack now. From next week Monday, let's keep this [WhatsApp] for immediate updates and messaging. Then slack can be where we focus discussions... I'll set up notification channels, issue trackers etc."

Group chat, 2026-08-09, 16:12 Beyers: status check — Pride Musvaire and Ryan James Noble had joined Slack; Fin K, Michael Hazel, Remi Bun Ooaasies, Rohan, and Ruchir still needed to join.

## Decision

WhatsApp is retained for immediate/operational messaging (quick updates, coordination pings). Slack is adopted as the primary channel for focused discussion and engineering coordination (notification channels, issue-tracker integration), effective 2026-08-10.

## Alternatives considered

- Discord — rejected by Ryan James Noble as too chaotic for this use.
- Stay on WhatsApp only — rejected: no contextual search, messages get lost, feature requests get lost ("This convo has been captured as Decision Record 14" — Beyers, 2026-08-05, 09:38).
- Slack only, dropping WhatsApp — not adopted; WhatsApp kept for immediate/operational updates.

## Consequences

- Positive: engineering discussion gains searchable history and (on a paid tier) a contextual search/AI bot.
- Negative: communication is now split across two tools.
- Risks: Slack free-tier message retention is limited (Ryan James Noble, 2026-08-07 13:31); a paid tier is under consideration, possibly funded via NPO grant or covered informally by Ryan James Noble (~R80). Migration was still incomplete as of 2026-08-09 (2 of 7 members joined), which is an execution risk, not a decision risk.

## Follow-up

- Actions: complete Slack onboarding for remaining members; confirm free vs. paid tier before relying on message retention for engineering history.
- Review date: once the team has fully migrated, or if free-tier retention limits become a problem.
