## Summary
The team aligned on building a modular community management portal for Africa Burn via improved digital infrastructure.

### System Vision and Scope
Leadership proposed a transparent digital portal to streamline theme camp operations and replace inefficient legacy systems. The technical architecture prioritizes modularity to support both mandatory organizational compliance and flexible community-led functionality.

### Technical Development Strategy
Developers demonstrated a robust software solution featuring integrated user profiles and extensible dashboards for camp leads. The project will initially focus on core integration points like placement tools and container management to demonstrate value to the organization.

### Organizational Pitch and Roadmap
The team decided to present a refined, demonstrative subset of the platform to the organization before September to secure support. Priorities include addressing logistical challenges like placement maps while maintaining a simple fallback option for non-technical users.
