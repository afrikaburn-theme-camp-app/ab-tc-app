> Decision records in this corpus record **operational** decisions: what we
> build, for whom, and why. They are not engineering HOW records — those live
> in [`docs/engineering-decisions/`](https://github.com/afrikaburn-theme-camp-app/ab-tc-app/blob/main/docs/engineering-decisions/README.md).
>
> We use them to document anything the working group will keep using for the
> long term. Some examples:
>
> - Processes
> - Product scope and gates
> - Integration posture with AfrikaBurn
> - Tooling choices that are operational, not implementation
