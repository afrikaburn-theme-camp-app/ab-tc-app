# Welcome

The Theme Camp App (Quagga Portal) is a management platform for theme camps, villages, collectives and creative projects attending AfrikaBurn or similar participatory Burner events. It is intended to reduce repetitive administration, improve participant onboarding, simplify annual registration and placement submissions, and give camps practical tools to manage their people, budgets, shifts, tickets, Work Access Passes and physical camp layouts.
